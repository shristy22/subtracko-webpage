SCREEN STORY

Each screen explains the product by showing consequence.

Dashboard

The first punch is the yearly number.

The dashboard opens with estimated financial drain, monthly spend, upcoming renewals, and active subscriptions. It is designed to make recurring payments feel real, not abstract.

New Obligation

Adding a subscription comes with immediate accountability.

When a user logs a service, SubTracko shows billing frequency, currency, renewal date, and the annual impact. The app frames a subscription as an obligation, not a harmless tap.

Cancellation

The app pushes the user through the last mile.

Cancellation is guided with explicit steps and a direct link to the provider flow. That reduces the gap between "I should cancel this" and actually doing it.

Victory + Graveyard

Savings are celebrated and retained as evidence.

The win state is shareable, and cancelled services move into the graveyard. Users can see what they already cut, how much it saves, and how many wasteful charges they stopped.
